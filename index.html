RiskDesk for Even Realities G2: try it in the browser

The real glasses app and the real backend logic, running in this tab on live Coinbase prices. Nothing to install, no keys, no money.

Menu open: click an item, or use ↑ ↓ and Tap. Double tap closes it.

Things to try

  • Tap on the positions page opens the risk page for each holding (24 h chart with entry / stop / take lines, money at the stop). Tap again for the next one; double tap goes back.
  • Menu → Halve BTC → the confirm page shows what is sold, at about which price, the fee and the position before → after. Tap arms it; you get 5 dots to tap again and take it back.
  • Say BTC stop at …, take profit at …: one confirm page with both legs. The stop then rests against the live bid; the positions page shows the distance to it.
  • Say a price that is off by 10x (the last example) or Buy half an ETH: the glasses refuse and say why, instead of guessing.
  • Double tap on the positions page switches English / 中文.

Keys: Enter tap, Esc double tap, ↑↓ scroll, m menu.

What is the same as on the glasses, and what is not

  • Same code. The page runs the unchanged glasses app (app/src/main.ts, which only lays out pages and forwards taps) and the unchanged desk (server/src/desk.ts: feed, paper ledger, actions, voice parser) that the Node backend serves.
  • Live data. Prices are the Coinbase Advanced Trade public WebSocket; the demo fills are priced at the real 1-minute candle of the time they claim. If the feed stalls, prices dim and say old.
  • Orders are paper here. With the backend, each confirmed action also sends a Coinbase sandbox Create Order; the sandbox does not accept browser calls, so this page books the paper fill at the live bid/ask only.
  • Voice is typed. On the glasses the mic audio goes to local faster-whisper; here your text is used as the transcript and goes through the same parser and safety checks.
  • Display is an approximation. Glyphs are placed at the firmware font's advance widths, but drawn with a browser font. The README screenshots are the simulator's real 576×288 buffer.
  • Your demo portfolio is kept in this browser only.
